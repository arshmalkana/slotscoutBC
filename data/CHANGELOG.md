# SlotScout public change log

Every release lists new rules, removed rules, changed boundaries, new closures and corrections (PRD 9.5).

## 2026-10 · October 2026 preview (Phase 0)

- First public preview of slotscout.ca.
- Live checks against the BC Data Catalogue for parks, ecological reserves, national parks, reserves, treaty lands, municipalities, electoral areas, Islands Trust areas, local parks, recreation sites and trails, wildfire restrictions, bans and fires, ParcelMap BC ownership, forest and permit roads, the Digital Road Atlas, cutblocks, motor vehicle closures, community watersheds, Crown, range and mineral tenures, transmission lines, pipelines, railways and buildings.
- Federal migratory bird sanctuaries and national wildlife areas from ECCC (CPCAD).
- District resource road notices read from each natural resource district's road safety page.
- Wildlife Act Closed Areas Regulation (consolidated to October 13, 2026): no region digitised yet. Every region shows "Cannot confirm" for closed areas.
- Local bylaw register created for 160 municipalities, 159 electoral areas and 13 local trust areas. None digitised yet; all show "Cannot confirm" with a link once the bylaw is found.
