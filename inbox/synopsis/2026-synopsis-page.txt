Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis
The [2026-2028 Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis (PDF, 13MB)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/sports-recreation-arts-and-culture/outdoor-recreation/fishing-and-hunting/hunting/regulations/hunting-trapping-synopsis.pdf) is a summary of the B.C. hunting and trapping regulations made under the [Wildlife Act](http://www.bclaws.ca/Recon/document/ID/freeside/00_96488_01), prepared for the convenience of hunters and trappers.
The regulations synopsis:
Sets out general hunting information
Summarizes important hunting regulations
Defines open seasons with maps indicating closed areas
The 2026-2028 Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis is effective from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2028.
For a summary of the changes and why they have been made, consult the [2026 Hunting and Trapping Regulations Changes Summary (PDF, 180KB)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/sports-recreation-arts-and-culture/outdoor-recreation/fishing-and-hunting/hunting/regulations/2026_hunting-trapping_regulation_changes_summary.pdf).
Corrections and updates: Changes to hunting and trapping regulations are updated in the online version of the synopsis and specific details are listed on [Hunting and Trapping Regulation Synopsis corrections and updates](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/sports-culture/recreation/fishing-hunting/hunting/regulations-synopsis/regulation-synopsis-corrections-and-updates).

Fraser Valley Special Hunting Areas
A [Fraser Valley Special Area Hunting Licence (PDF, 21MB)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/sports-recreation-arts-and-culture/outdoor-recreation/fishing-and-hunting/hunting/regulations/fraser_valley_special_hunting_area_map_brochure.pdf) is required to hunt anywhere within provincial Management Unit 2-4 and parts of Management Unit 2-8 within Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows, Mission and Coquitlam.
The Fraser Valley Special Area Hunting Licence map brochure was last published in the 2023-2024 licence year. Updating and printing the brochure to reflect current municipal Discharge of Firearms Bylaw (DFB) boundaries is not currently scheduled. Hunters should be aware that there may have been significant DFB boundary changes and amendments enacted by local Councils since 2023-2024.
Hunters are also reminded it is their responsibility to ensure they are very familiar with local DFB boundaries at all times when afield.
The current DFB boundaries for each city and municipality can be obtained from their Clerk’s Office. Request a copy of the wording of the bylaw itself and also the Appendix “A” map of the actual DFB boundaries.
Hunting in parks
For information on hunting in provincial parks, visit [BC Parks' fishing and hunting guide](https://bcparks.ca/plan-your-trip/visit-responsibly/fishing-hunting-guide/). To see if the park you are planning to visit allows hunting, [check the park’s webpage](https://bcparks.ca/find-a-park).
