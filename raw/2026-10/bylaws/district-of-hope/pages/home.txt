Ready, Set, Vote!

🗳️ Today’s the Day, Hope!

Voting is open until 8:00 p.m. today at the Hope Royal Canadian Legion Branch 228 Hall, 344 Fort Street.

District of Hope voters will elect six Councillors.

Bring two pieces of identification, including at least one with your signature. Photo ID is not required.

📬 If you’re voting by mail ballot, your completed package must be received by 8:00 p.m. today.

For candidate names, voter eligibility, mail ballot instructions and the full Notice of Election by Voting, visit:
