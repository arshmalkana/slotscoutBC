District bylaws establish the rules and standards that help keep Hope safe, welcoming, and well-managed for residents, businesses, and visitors.
BYLAW DIRECTORY

The bylaws provided on this page are for information purposes only. While every effort is made to ensure the information is current and accurate, the District of Hope does not guarantee the completeness or legal accuracy of online bylaw documents.

Where applicable, amendment bylaws have been consolidated into the original bylaw for ease of reference. The official signed versions of all bylaws are maintained by the District of Hope.

Individuals wishing to rely on a bylaw for legal, business, or other formal purposes should contact the Corporate Services Department to confirm that the bylaw is current and in force. Certified copies of official bylaws are available upon request.

For frequently asked questions about waste management, including garbage, recycling, organics, and collection services, please visit the Waste Management page.

If you are unable to find the bylaw you are looking for, please contact the District of Hope for assistance.

Questions regarding bylaw enforcement, community standards, or regulatory concerns can be directed to Bylaw Services at 604-869-5671 or bylaw@hope.ca.
