Service Requests

See it? Report it! We take great pride in providing quality service to our community. Use the form below to submit non-emergency concerns and comments related to City services and bylaws.

If it's an emergency:

For urgent emergencies requiring police, fire or ambulance services, please dial 911 immediately
For emergencies relating to sewer, water, environmental spills, streets or lanes, contact Public Works at 604.927.5496 (office hours) or 604.927.3111 (after hours)
Additional emergency contact information can be found here.
