Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000

The Zoning Bylaw (ZB) assigns all properties in the City to specific zones. Each zone in the ZB includes regulations on what types of land uses are permitted and restrictions on buildings and structures (e.g., height, setbacks, floor area). Other development requirements (e.g., parking, landscaping, paving, amenity areas) are also set in the ZB.

The complete copy of "Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000" is provided for information purposes only, and the City of Chilliwack will not be responsible for its validity or completeness.

Contact the Planning Department for inquiries regarding the general content of the Zoning Bylaw or current zoning of a property.

Contact the Legislative Services Department at (604) 793-2986 or clerks@chilliwack.com for:

Certified copy of the consolidated (full) bylaw
Copy of an amendment bylaw.
