                                        City of Chilliwack

                                         Bylaw No. 2740

                            A bylaw to regulate the use of firearms


WHEREAS Section 8(5) of the Community Charter empowers Council, by bylaw, to regulate or
prohibit the discharging of firearms and provides that such regulations may be different for
different areas within the municipality;

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the City of Chilliwack in open meeting assembled enacts as
follows:

1.     This Bylaw may be cited as the “Firearms Regulation Bylaw 2001, No. 2740”.

2.     “Firearms Regulation Bylaw 1994, No. 2105” and amendments thereto are hereby
       repealed.

INTERPRETATION

3.     In this bylaw:

       “City” means the City of Chilliwack;
       “Bow” means a longbow or crossbow as defined in the Wildlife Act;
       "Firearm” means a gun, shotgun, pistol, air pistol, air gun, air rifle, pellet gun, spring gun,
              or any other weapon from which any shot, bullet or other projectile can be
              discharged, but does not include a starting pistol in connection with an athletic
              event where blank ammunition is used.”
PROHIBITION

4.     No person shall discharge a firearm or Bow within any part of the City that is shown on
       Schedule “A” of this bylaw as "closed to the discharge of firearms".

RESPONSIBILITY OF PARENT OR GUARDIAN

5.     Every parent or guardian of a person who is under 16 years of age and living at the home
       of the parent or guardian shall ensure that the person does not discharge a firearm
       contrary to the provisions of this bylaw.

EXEMPTIONS

6.    Section 4 of this bylaw does not apply to:

      (1)    a person engaged in a farm operation who discharges a firearm for the purpose of
             protecting crops or livestock from injury or destruction by animals or birds; or

      (2)    a person engaged in the slaughter of livestock, where such livestock is legally
             kept according to the terms, conditions and provisions of all applicable City
             bylaws in force from time to time; or

      (3)    a peace officer, a conservation officer, or an employee of the City, the
             Government of British Columbia, the Government of Canada or the Society for
             the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals, who discharges a firearm in the lawful
             performance of that person’s duties;

      (4)    the use of a firearm or bow where the use is part of an authorized sporting event
             under the supervision of a local, national or international sports governing body;

      (5)    the lawful operation of a shooting or target range;

      (6)    the use of a Bow on private land when the person using the Bow has received the
             prior permission of the owner of said land and the Bow is used to shoot an arrow
             or other projectile at a stationary inanimate target, and the person using the Bow
             exercises care for the safety of other persons or property.

7.    Every person listed in Section 6 above, when discharging a firearm or using a bow, shall
      do so only with due regard for the safety and security of other residents, visitors and
      occupants of the City.

8.    Every person who violates or breaches, or who causes or allows to be violated or
      breached, any of the provisions of this bylaw shall be guilty of an offence against this
      bylaw.

9.    Any person guilty of an infraction of this bylaw shall be liable, upon summary
      conviction, to a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100.00) and not more than the
      maximum allowed by the Offence Act, as amended.

10.   If any portion of this bylaw is for any reason found invalid by a court of competent
      jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of the
      bylaw.
