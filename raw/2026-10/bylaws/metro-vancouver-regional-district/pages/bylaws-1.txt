​Bylaws are enacted by three of Metro Vancouver’s legal entities:

Metro Vancouver Regional District (MVRD), formerly called the Greater Vancouver Regional District (GVRD);​​
Greater Vancouver Water District (GVWD); and
​​Greater Vancouver Sewerage and Drainage District (GVS&DD)​.

An amendment bylaw amends an original bylaw, and an unofficial consolidation combines an original bylaw with all amendments made to that bylaw over time. An unofficial consolidation serves as a reference tool for convenience purposes only and is not a legal document.

To find a bylaw:

Search by keyword using quotes. For example: “Election” or “Remuneration”.

Search by bylaw number using quotes. For example, “1102”.
Search using the filters located on the right-hand side of this webpage.
Search using Boolean operators, such as AND, OR, and NOT alongside keywords.

This webpage contains frequently requested bylaws.

​​Disclaimer: Bylaws posted on this webpage are provided for information purposes only. Although every effort is made to ensure the accuracy and completeness of online bylaws, they should not be used in place of a certified true copy of a bylaw. Metro Vancouver will not be liable or responsible for damages of any kind arising out of the use of online bylaws. If you have questions about Metro Vancouver bylaws or would like to request a true certified copy of a bylaw, please contact Board and Information Services at ​BIS-Secretariat@metrovancouver.org​​.
