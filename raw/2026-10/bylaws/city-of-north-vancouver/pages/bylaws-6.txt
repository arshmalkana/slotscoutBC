Business Licence Bylaws

When applying for a business licence, ensure that you're familiar with the City of North Vancouver's Business Licence Bylaw, which outlines fee structures and the renewal process.

The nature of your licence fees will vary depending on the nature of your business, and is determined by the number of people working, the amount of space occupied, or other criteria as specified in the Business Licence Bylaw.

A business licence is valid for one calendar year, and automatic renewal notices are sent to each business owner every December.
