Bylaw Enforcement

The City of Port Moody's Bylaw Division enforces our bylaws. We work with the Port Moody Police Department, Port Moody Fire Rescue, health agencies, and property owners to keep our community safe.

Disputing or paying a municipal ticket

To dispute a municipal ticket, please complete a Quash Request form. Please note that disputes will only be considered if there is a valid reason. Municipal tickets will not be dismissed for such reasons as: you have never received a ticket for this violation in the past, you were unaware of the bylaw, nobody else has received a ticket for a similar violation, the bylaw seems unfair. 

You can pay a municipal ticket by mail or in person:

8:30am - 5pm Monday-Friday (closed on statutory holidays)
City Hall 2nd Floor - 100 Newport Dr
Port Moody BC  V3H 3E1

Applications for Sound Level Bylaw relaxation
Temporary relaxation from sound bylaw decibel levels

Submit application 5 business days before your intended start date.

Pursuant to Section 11 of the City of Port Moody Sound Level Bylaw No. 1399 where it is impossible or impractical to comply with the prescribed decibel levels specified in Section 6, the General Manager of Planning and Development Services may give written approval to carry out work that is found to be necessary outside of the prescribed decibel levels.

Apply here for a temporary relaxation from sound decibel level restrictions of the Sound Level Bylaw

Temporary Relaxation from sound bylaw hours of construction

Submit application 5 business days before your intended start date.

Pursuant to Section 4.2 of the City of Port Moody Sound Level Bylaw No. 1399 where it is impossible or impractical to comply with the construction hours specified in Section 4.2, the General Manager of Planning and Development Services may give written approval to carry out work that is found to be necessary outside of the designated construction hours.

Apply here for a temporary relaxation from the construction hour restrictions of the Sound Level Bylaw
