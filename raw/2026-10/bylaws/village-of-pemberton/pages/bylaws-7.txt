Report an Issue
Working together to keep Pemberton safe, clean, and well-maintained

Noticed something that needs attention around town? Whether it’s a bylaw concern, a maintenance issue, or something else you think we should know about, this is the place to start.

Your reports help us respond quickly and efficiently to issues that affect daily life in our community. We review every submission and forward each to the appropriate department.
This form is for non-emergency concerns only.

For emergencies, call 911.
For after-hours Operations emergencies (like water main breaks), call 1-855-215-4941.
Bylaw Enforcement Issues

These issues are related to municipal bylaws and may include:

Parking violations
Dog complaints
Water restriction breaches
Unsightly properties
Excessive noise
Other bylaw-related concerns

Our bylaw team works through a tiered approach to encourage compliance:

Education to raise awareness
Voluntary compliance on a case-by-case basis
Formal enforcement, which may include issuing tickets or legal action



Report a Bylaw Issue

Tips for Reporting a Bylaw Issue
Maintenance Issues

Public Works and maintenance concerns might include:

Snow and ice on roads or sidewalks
Parks and groundskeeping
Streetlight outages
Water and sewer service problems
Boulevard or roadside maintenance

Report a Maintenance Issue





After-Hours Emergencies

If you have an emergency outside regular office hours, please call 1-855-215-4941 for assistance.

Pemberton & District Community Centre Concerns

If your concern relates to the Pemberton & District Community Centre, please email us.
