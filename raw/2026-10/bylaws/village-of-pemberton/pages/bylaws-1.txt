Helping guide land use and development

Zoning sets the rules for how land, buildings, and properties can be used and developed within the Village of Pemberton. Through the Zoning Bylaw, land is divided into different zones (such as residential, commercial, and industrial) and outlines what is permitted in each area.

These regulations help ensure that development is compatible, supports community goals, and contributes to a well-planned and livable environment. Zoning also addresses details like building height, setbacks, density, and parking requirements, providing clear expectations for property owners, developers, and residents.

Zoning Bylaw

The Zoning Bylaw establishes different zones for agricultural, residential, commercial, and industrial areas. A zoning bylaw is a tool that defines how land may be used or developed. Regulations in a zone limit the uses, density, size, height and location of buildings on a property.

Review Zoning Bylaw No. 832

Zoning Bylaw Amendment (Rezoning)

If your proposal doesn’t match the permitted uses or regulations of the current zoning, a Zoning Bylaw Amendment (rezoning) is required. The proposed change must align with the Official Community Plan (OCP).
