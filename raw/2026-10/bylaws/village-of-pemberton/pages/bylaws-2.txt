Guiding long-term growth and change

The Official Community Plan (OCP) sets out a long-term vision for how the Village of Pemberton will grow and evolve over time. It provides policy direction on land use, housing, transportation, the environment, and community well-being, helping to guide future planning and development decisions.

The OCP reflects community priorities and values, and is used by Council and staff to evaluate projects, update bylaws, and plan for infrastructure and services. While it does not regulate development directly, it establishes the framework that informs other tools, such as zoning and development permits.

Official Community Plan

The Official Community Plan (OCP) contains long-term objectives and policies that guide decision-making regarding land use, development, services, amenities, and infrastructure. The OCP also establishes development permit areas and heritage conservation areas.
