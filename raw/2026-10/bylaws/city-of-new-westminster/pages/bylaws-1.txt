Certified copies of the original bylaws should be consulted for all interpretation and application of the bylaws. To obtain certified copies of original bylaws or to verify that this is the most up to date copy of the bylaw, please contact Legislative Services at 604-527-4523 or send your request by email to legislativeservices@newwestcity.ca.
