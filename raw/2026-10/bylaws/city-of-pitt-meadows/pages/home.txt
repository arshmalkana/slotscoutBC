Halloween at the Fire Hall

Oct 31 2026 | 6 - 8pm

Pitt Meadows firefighters will be handing out candy to trick-or-treaters on Halloween night, Saturday, October 31, from 6 to 8 p.m. at the Pitt Meadows Fire Hall. Don’t miss out on a spooktacular treat from your local heroes!

Pitt Meadows Firefighter Charitable Society Pumpkin Smash

Nov 1 2026 | 12 - 3pm

Join the Pitt Meadows Firefighters Charitable Society for a fun-filled day at their Pumpkin Smash! Bring the whole family and watch your pumpkins take the plunge from the fire hall hose tower!

Call for artists: Handcrafted Gifts

The Pitt Meadows Art Gallery is seeking local artists and artisans for Handcrafted Gifts, its annual holiday exhibition. The show runs November 7–December 23 and features original, giftable artwork and fine crafts. Apply by Sunday, October 11.

Visit the Pitt Meadows Art Galley to experience contemporary Indigenous art from the Cedar Sage and Sweetgrass Art Collective, whose works draw upon traditional teachings while embracing contemporary perspectives and creative practices.
