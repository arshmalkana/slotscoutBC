Transport Canada – Sewage Discharge

  1. Home
  2. Transport Canada – Sewage Discharge

Transport Canada enforces strict regulations to ensure compliance with sewage discharge requirements. The regulations prohibit the discharge of raw sewage directly into the water, which can harm fish and bivalve shellfish and pose a threat to human health. Violations can result in fines up to $1 million or imprisonment for up to 18 months. For more information on regulatory requirements, individuals can contact Transport Canada’s Office of Boating Safety.
