Complying with Sewage Discharge Regulations


Transport Canada regulations prohibit the discharge of raw sewage directly into the water. Your sewage discharge
affects fish, crabs and bivalve shellfish (oysters, clams and mussels), causing contamination which can threaten human
health. A violation of this regulation can carry a fine of up to $1 million or up to 18 months of imprisonment, or both.

Here’s what you can do to comply:
        OPTION 1                                     OPTION 2                                      OPTION 3
        Use a MARINE SANITATION                      Use a HOLDING TANK and                        Use a TEMPORARY STORAGE
        DEVICE, or discharge 3nm                     PUMP-OUT STATION                              like a porta-potty
        offshore                                     (Visit www.ahoybc.com/map to
                                                     find one near you)




   Contact Transport Canada’s Office of Boating Safety at 604-666-2681 or email: TC.PAC.TM.OBS-BSN.TC@TC.GC.CA about regulatory requirements.
   Vessel Pollution and Dangerous Chemicals Regulations are online at: http://www.laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69/page-1.html.
