VILLAGE OF BELCARRA
Firearms Control Bylaw

BELCARRA Bylaw No. 498, 2016
1979

A bylaw to regulate and prohibit the discharge of firearms
WHEREAS Council may, by bylaw, regulate and prohibit the discharge of firearms;

NOW THEREFORE the Council Village of Belcarra, in open meeting assembled, enacts
as follows:

Title

1. This Bylaw may be cited as the “Village of Belcarra Firearms Control Bylaw
No. 498, 2016”.

Definitions
Zz. In this Bylaw:

a. “enforcement officer’ means a person authorized to enforce this Bylaw.

b. “firearm” means a barrelled weapon from which any shot, bullet or other
projectile can be discharged and that is capable of causing serious bodily injury
or death to a person, and includes any frame or receiver of such a barrelled
weapon and anything that can be adapted for use as a firearm.

c. “bow” means a bow as defined in the Wildlife Act.

Prohibition

3. No person shall fire or discharge, or permit to be fired or discharged, any firearm
within the Village of Belcarra.

4. No person shall fire or discharge, permit to be fired or discharged, a bow within

the Village of Belcarra.
Exemptions
5. Section 3 does not apply to a person who is:
a) a peace Officer;
b) an officer under the Wildlife Act;
C) a fishery officer under the Fisheries Act; or,
d) otherwise authorized by a provincial or federal enactment to possess and
discharge firearms in the course of his or her duties, and who is engaged
in, and acting in only accordance with, the lawful performance of his or her

duties.

Enforcement
e) This Bylaw may be enforced by any police officer of the Royal Canadian
Mounted Police or any other Peace Officer with jurisdiction in British

Columbia.

f) No person shall obstruct, or attempt to obstruct, an enforcement officer in
the administration or enforcement of this Bylaw.

g) Every person who contravenes a provision of this Bylaw commits an
offense, and each contravention constitutes a separate offence.

h) Any person guilty of an offense under this Bylaw is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of not less than five hundred dollars ($500.00) and not
more than five thousand dollars ($5,000.00).

Repeal

i) Village of Belcarra Firearms Control Bylaw, 186, No. 98 is repealed.
