Community Safety Team (formerly Bylaw Enforcement)

Langley City’s Community Safety team helps keep our community safe, respectful and welcoming by responding to concerns, supporting voluntary compliance and enforcing City bylaws when needed. 

Community Safety Officers (formerly Bylaw Enforcement Officers) work with residents, businesses and community partners to resolve issues, share information and connect people directly with the right services when extra support is needed. 

A new approach to community safety

Learn how the City is creating a coordinated one-stop approach for neighbourhood questions and transitioning to Community Safety Officers based on the Citizens’ Assembly recommendations.

Read Media Release
View Citizens' Assembly Recommendations
What We Do
Work with partner agencies on community safety and well-being concerns 
Connect people with appropriate services and resources when needed 
Respond to bylaw concerns and service requests 
Help residents and businesses understand City bylaws 
Encourage voluntary compliance through education and outreach 
Patrol City streets, parks and public spaces 
Address parking and other bylaw-related matters
