Bylaw Service Request

If you have a concern in regards to a bylaw infraction occurring within the City of Abbotsford, complete the form below.

When submitting a request for service, we ask that you provide the exact address where the alleged bylaw infraction has occurred if possible. If an address is not available, please fill in the free form section with a detailed description of the location.

Please be assured that the City keeps the identity of every service requester confidential.
