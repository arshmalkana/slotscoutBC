Frequently requested bylaws list

Please note that bylaws are continually being amended and consolidated. Bylaws and the frequently requested page are updated as they are adopted by Council.  For the most current information regarding bylaws and bylaws in process, please contact Legislative Services.

Contact

For bylaw questions relating to parking, noise, business licensing, animal control, garbage, and property maintenance, please contact:
Bylaw Services
604-935-8280
bylawservices@whistler.ca

For zoning and land use questions, please contact:
Planning Department
604-935-8170
planning@whistler.ca

For all other bylaw questions, please contact:
Legislative Services
corporate@whistler.ca
