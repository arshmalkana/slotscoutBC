Tree Management

Coquitlam has a tree management bylaw (PDF) designed to protect trees, regulate cutting and ensure replacement of trees removed. The bylaw applies to all private properties in Coquitlam.

See the City's Permits and Application Portal to submit an online tree removal application or  contact Parks Customer Service for any questions.

City Owned Trees - Boulevard and Parks

Information related to City owned trees within Parks and City boulevards.

Private Property Trees

The City of Coquitlam does not regulate the trimming of trees on private property as long as the trimming does not violate the City’s Tree Management Bylaw.

Tree Cutting Permit

Find out if you require a tree cutting permit for tree removal and replacement on private property. All information is related to the Tree Maintenance Bylaw 4091, 2010.

Trees & Development

When redeveloping a property, impacts to neighbouring trees must also be considered. The bird nesting window in the City of Coquitlam exists between March 1st and August 31st. Tree removal during this period must ensure no nests or nesting birds are disturbed.
