Bylaw Enforcement & Tickets
Learn about City of Burnaby bylaws, review current enforceable bylaws or pay a ticket.

Bylaws are formalized rules made by Council that keep our city safe and peaceful. The most common bylaws in Burnaby are related to licensing, property use, building, climate action, waste disposal, graffiti, animal control and parking enforcement.

Bylaw enforcement officers support and uphold City bylaws while ensuring public safety, maintaining community standards and managing behavioural and nuisance issues. Our bylaw enforcement practices are guided by the City's Bylaw Enforcement Policy. 

Enforcement officers patrolling Burnaby’s parks carry out their duties wearing body cameras, which help ensure the accurate collection of evidence and increase public trust and transparency. Our use of body-worn cameras is guided by the City’s Body-Worn Camera Policy.

Be aware of impersonation scams

Impersonation scams may involve text messages, emails, QR codes, letters, phone calls or websites that appear legitimate but are designed to trick individuals or businesses into sending money or sharing sensitive information. These scams often use official looking logos, familiar names and urgent language to pressure recipients into acting quickly. Learn how to protect yourself.
