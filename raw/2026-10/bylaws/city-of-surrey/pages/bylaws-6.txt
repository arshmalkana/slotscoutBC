The Official Community Plan (OCP) is a comprehensive, overarching document that directs growth, development and environmental, social and economic objectives over a 30 year period. Surrey’s OCP is aligned with Metro Vancouver’s Regional Growth Strategy as well as the goals of the City’s Sustainability Charter, and therefore provides a fully integrated vision and policy direction for Surrey residents, businesses and visitors.

Surrey 2050

The current Official Community Plan, Surrey 2050, was adopted by Council in June 2026 and reflects the goals and priorities of residents across the city.

Surrey 2050 is a blueprint that will guide how the city grows and evolves over the next 30 years as it moves toward supporting a population expected to reach one million residents. 

Read the Official Community Plan: Surrey 2050.
