                           Ministry of Forests


                       ROAD RESTRICTIONS NOTICE April 10, 2026
                          for the Stuart Nechako Forest District
                       This notice becomes effective April 13, 2026,
                                      at 12:00:01 am.
                                            Bulletin #3

    Be advised, pursuant to Section 6 (2) (e) of the Forest Service Road Use Regulation, road
    restrictions will be implemented on Forest Service Roads (FSRs) within the Stuart Nechako
                                      Forest District as follows:


              All Forest Service Roads within the Stuart Nechako Forest District

                     Effective April 13, 2026, at 12:00:01am – restricted

                                70% Highway legal axle loading.


  Road users must monitor roads for damage. If any subgrade damage occurs, activity
                              must cease immediately.

      Use of the FSR’s for the following are not restricted:

         •   Road maintenance activities
         •   Tree planting activities.
         •   Grocery, fuel, and other essential deliveries to communities.


               Restrictions subject to change on short notice.
      The District will continue to monitor road conditions and may change restrictions.




Jill Park RPF
District Manager
