                         ROAD RESTRICTIONS NOTICE June 2, 2026
                            for the Stuart Nechako Forest District
                  This notice becomes effective Tuesday June 2, 2026, at
                                        12:01AM



    Be advised, pursuant to Section 6 (2) (e) of the Forest Service Road Use Regulation, road
    restrictions will be implemented on Forest Service Roads (FSRs) within the Stuart Nechako
                                      Forest District as follows:


                             The Kluskus FSR and Holy Cross FSR

       Effective June 2, 2026, at 12:01 AM – Unrestricted – 100% Legal Axle Loading




 Restrictions subject to change on short notice.

 The District will monitor road conditions and may change restrictions.

 *70% signs may be in place for several days after restrictions are lifted, until they are removed
