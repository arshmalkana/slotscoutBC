Driftwood FSR 15km Rebuild

OVERVIEW
The Ministry of Forests (FOR) will be contracting out work to rebuild
a section of the Driftwood FSR north of Fort St James from 15.1km
to 16.7km. The current road configuration does not meet the
desired road design and must be re-worked to meet the required
vertical alignment.

SCHEDULE
The project will take place from July 6 - July 24, 2026.


IMPACTS ON ROAD USERS
Minor delays may be encountered but traffic will be able to
bypass the work area either along the FSR itself, or by
using a short detour route.
