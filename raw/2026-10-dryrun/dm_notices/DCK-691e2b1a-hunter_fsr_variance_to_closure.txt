 December 16, 2025

 Notice is given under Section 22.2(3) of the Forest and Range Practices Act,

 Effective immediately and until further notice a variance to the notice of closure of
 the Hunter Creek Forest Service Road FSR (6086.01) issued December 15, 2025, is
 given to the road users listed below for the purposes stipulated.

 The variance is to provide access and use of the FSR beyond structure CK-2157 and
 is limited to Hunter Creek Hydro Power L.P. Logging Ltd. (holder of Road Use
 Permit # 6086-16-02). Structure CK-2157 remains CLOSED and requires further
 assessment before any vehicle crossings can be authorized.
