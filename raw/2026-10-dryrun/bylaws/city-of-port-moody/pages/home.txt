Drop-in ice sports

Join us on the ice for a variety of activities, including parent and child skates, adult-only sessions, drop-in hockey, ring-stick-and-puck, Friday night youth skates, and more!

Volunteer to remove invasive plants

Help us protect our parks and green spaces by removing invasive plants along the Shoreline Trail. Join us for one of our monthly events! Learn more at portmoody.ca/environment.

Be bear aware

We live in bear country! Managing access to garbage and other attractants is the most important action to take to keep people and bears safe in our community.

Roadwork and construction

Find information about major roadwork, construction, park upgrades, and development projects that may impact traffic, trails, or access to public spaces in Port Moody.

Report a problem

Report a problem online or by phone. For concerns or hazards related to graffiti, trees, roads, water and sewer, and more you can use our online form or call our 24/7 service line at 604.469.4574.
