Secondary Suites
Zoning
Zoning 5942 (pre 12000)
Bylaw Enforcement Notice (BEN)

Review secondary suite fees and see how to register your secondary suite in Surrey.

A secondary suite is a self-contained dwelling unit, meaning an area that is used or can be used independently for living and sleeping purposes and includes cooking facilities and a bathroom.  Secondary suites may be located within a single-family home, duplex, triplex, or fourplex, or in a separate building or structure (also known as a secondary dwelling) on the same lot as the listed types of housing.

All secondary suites in Surrey must be registered. To register, complete the Secondary Suite Declaration Form and submit the signed form to Property & Payment Services to propertytaxesandutilities@surrey.ca or in person at Surrey City Hall.

A $1,000 fee will be billed to the property for each unregistered secondary suite if the City becomes aware of a suite that is not registered.

Check if a suite is registered using our Secondary Suite Fee Web Inquiry tool.

Secondary suite annual payments

The following fees are set by the Secondary Suite Service Fee Bylaw and Waste Management Regulations and Charges Bylaw, respectively.

Fees can be paid by cash, cheque or debit. Credit cards are not accepted.

Secondary suite building regulations

Secondary suites are permitted in accordance with the Zoning Bylaw, 

Learn where and how you can build, install or apply for a legal secondary suite.

To align with recent provincial housing legislation, the City adopted changes to its zoning bylaw for Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing, Learn more about zoning for small-scale, multi-unit housing.

Requirements

To legalize or install a new or existing secondary suite, a homeowner must:

meet the conditions of zoning,
have necessary building permits,
meet the standards of the BC Building Code,
pay secondary suite utility and service fees, and
submit a building permit application if previously a building permit to authorize the existing secondary suite is not obtained to demonstrate compliance.

Learn where and how you can build, install or apply for a legal secondary suite.

Complaints & enforcement

Submit complaints about secondary suites online.

Once a complaint is received, a City Bylaw Officer conducts an inspection. If the Officer determines that a secondary suite (or more than one secondary suite) exists where a secondary suite isn’t not permitted, the bylaw is explained to the property owner, as well as what must be done to bring the property into compliance. Follow up inspections are conducted to ensure compliance with the bylaw.

The bylaw doesn’t address who the occupant of a secondary suite is or whether the suite is occupied at all. This means that whether or not the secondary suite is occupied by family, rented out or vacant makes no difference in determining if a secondary suite exists. The existence of the cooking equipment and bathroom are the only considerations as to whether or not there is a secondary suite.

Secondary suite removal

Once you’ve removed any secondary suite, you can request an inspection with Bylaws by calling 604-591-4516, and pressing Option 2.

Before calling for an inspection, you must first:

Ensure an electrical permit is issued before completing any electrical work
Remove any stove(s) and other cooking equipment
Disconnect any electrical outlets for stove(s) and cover them with drywall or a metal plate
Gas appliance removal requires permits from Technical Safety BC and must be done by a licensed contractor. Contact Technical Safety BC
Remove any hood fan
Remove the breaker for any stove from the electrical panel
Other applicable requirements as per the Secondary Suite Service Fee By-law, 2011, No. 17356
