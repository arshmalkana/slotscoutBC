Learn more about the heritage sites that interest you. Heritage Bylaws outline how buildings in Surrey receive and retain their heritage status. 

This page provides a complete list of Heritage Designation Bylaws and Heritage Revitalization Agreement Bylaws with links. Listings are by adoption date, with the most recent Heritage Designation Bylaws and Heritage Revitalization Agreements at the end.

This is the place to start if you know the address, name of the heritage site that interests you, or just want a general overview.
