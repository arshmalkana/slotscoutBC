Bylaws
The Village of Anmore’s bylaws establish the rules and regulations that help guide the community, including matters such as land use, property maintenance, animals, noise, parking and other municipal services.

On this page, you can learn how to report a concern to our bylaws department, or browse specific bylaws to learn how they apply.

A selection of Village bylaws is listed below.

Simply click on the bylaw you wish to view. Note: Online copies of bylaws may not include recent amendments and the list below does not include all Village bylaws. Official copies are available from the Village Hall.

Submitting a Bylaw Complaint

All bylaw complaints must be submitted via the Village’s official complaint form.

Not sure who to contact about a concern? 

Visit our Reporting a Concern page for information on where to direct your concern, including bylaw complaints and matters that fall outside the bylaws department.

The Bylaw Department can be contacted by phone at 604-828-4987 during regular business hours.

Complaints received outside of working hours will not be reviewed until the next shift. The Bylaw Department does not respond to text messages.
