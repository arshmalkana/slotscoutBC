Bylaw Services

Bylaws are established to protect a safe and livable environment for those who live, work, learn, and play in the City of Port Coquitlam. Bylaw Officers aim to foster voluntary compliance through information, education, and respectful enforcement, considering all perspectives and the broader impact on the community.

Our Bylaw Officers work closely with other law enforcement agencies, government bodies, community groups, and social service organizations to uphold safety, maintain standards, and address behavioural and nuisance concerns to preserve residents' quality of life. We respond to public, agency complaints and proactively identify community issues to support a thriving Port Coquitlam.

Bylaw Officers are on duty daily from 7:30am-10:00pm.
