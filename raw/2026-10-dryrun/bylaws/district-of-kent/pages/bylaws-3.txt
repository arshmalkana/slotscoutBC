NOTICE OF MUNICIPAL TAX EXEMPTIONS

In accordance with Section 227 of the Community Charter, the District of Kent gives notice of its intention to adopt the bylaw cited as “2027 Non-Profit Organizations Permissive Tax Exemption Bylaw No. 1772, 2026” at the Regular Council meeting scheduled on Monday, October 26, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers, Municipal Hall, 7170 Cheam Avenue, Agassiz, BC.


This bylaw will establish a tax exemption from land and improvements for the year 2027 for the property listed below. An estimate of the amount of taxes that would be imposed on the property if it was not exempt for the year in which the proposed bylaw is to take effect and the following two years are described as follows:
