Bylaw Compliance

The Bylaw Compliance Strategy supports Council’s Strategic Plan, which outlines a commitment to Organizational Excellence and Integrity encompassing consistent governance and aligned operations. It sets out a path for how the City will deliver its bylaw compliance and enforcement services with a strong focus on public awareness, consistency, fairness, and commitment to service quality.

Bylaw Compliance Strategy

The Bylaw Compliance Strategy will align with the strategic direction adopted by Abbotsford City Council, will provide enhanced customer service and achieve the following four primary objectives:

Builds upon Council adopted Bylaw Compliance Policy which prioritizes how calls for service are triaged
Customer centric and community focused
Service delivery model aligns with current and future service demands
Consistency and clarity in responses to non-compliance
